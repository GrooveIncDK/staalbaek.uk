Our Story

Staalbaek Ltd was set up in October 2022 as the UK arm of the organisation Stålbæk Holding ApS, for the expansion of the platform Your Footprint — Europe's most comprehensive CO2e calculation tool for the agricultural sector.

Developed in conjunction with Denmark's first CO2e beef farm, Stålbæk Gård ApS.
